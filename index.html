✅ Before you start — requirements

  • Age 21+ with a full Ontario G licence
  • SIN and legal right to work in Canada
  • Background check — driving record and criminal history screening
  • Vehicle: 4-door, generally 10 years old or newer, in good condition
  • Safety inspection: an Ontario Safety Standards Certificate from a licensed inspection station (renewed yearly)
  • Insurance: your own auto policy; the platforms add commercial coverage while you are online
  • Toronto PTC licence: required to pick up in Toronto — the platform arranges it with the City after your background check (you don't apply yourself)
  • Winter tires are required in Toronto during the winter season

📝 How signup works (same flow on all three apps)

  1. Start through a referral link above so the welcome offer is attached to your account.
  2. Create your account and upload documents: licence, vehicle registration, insurance, and a profile photo.
  3. Book and pass the vehicle safety inspection; upload the certificate.
  4. Complete the background check (usually a few days).
  5. Wait for your Toronto PTC licence to be issued through the platform, then go online and take your first trip.
  6. Repeat the same document set on the other two apps — driving on multiple apps cuts your waiting time between trips.

📍 Driving tips — where and when to drive

Hotspots that work

  • Pearson Airport (YYZ): use the designated rideshare waiting lot and the official queue — never wait on airport roads
  • Downtown Toronto: after concerts, games and events at Scotiabank Arena, Rogers Centre and the theatre district
  • Square One (Mississauga), Scarborough Town Centre, Yorkdale and Vaughan Mills — evenings and weekends
  • GO stations at morning and evening rush — commuters heading to and from trains

Habits that pay

  • Drive peak hours: weekday 6–9 am and 4–8 pm, Friday and Saturday nights, and bad-weather days
  • Run 2–3 apps at once and pause the others while on a trip
  • Highway 407 can save real time, but confirm the toll will be covered before choosing it — toll policies differ by platform
  • Keep the car clean, carry phone charging cables, and be polite — ratings protect your account

💰 Costs, HST and taxes

  • You are an independent contractor: track kilometres, fuel/charging, car washes, phone and inspection fees as business expenses
  • Rideshare drivers in Canada generally must register for and charge HST on their services — set aside a portion of every payout
  • Keep money aside for income tax; there is no employer withholding it for you
  • Earnings are never guaranteed — they depend on demand, hours and location

❓ Frequently asked questions

What do I need to drive in Toronto?
Age 21+, a full Ontario G licence, a SIN and right to work in Canada, a background check, a qualifying 4-door vehicle with a safety certificate, and a Toronto PTC licence arranged through the platform.

What is the PTC licence?
The City of Toronto's Private Transportation Company driver licence. You don't apply for it yourself — the platform submits it after your background screening clears.

Why sign up through a referral link?
The new-driver welcome offer only attaches when you sign up through a referral link before registering. Signing up directly in the app means no welcome bonus.

Can I drive on all three apps?
Yes — most GTA drivers run two or three apps to reduce waiting time. Use the same documents for each signup.

I live outside the GTA — can I still use these links?
For Uber and Lyft, yes, anywhere in Canada: referrals only count within the same country, and the bonus amount follows your signup city. Signups outside Canada do not earn a referral bonus.

How much will I earn?
It varies with hours, demand and location. Peak times pay best. Treat it as variable business income and budget for HST and taxes.
